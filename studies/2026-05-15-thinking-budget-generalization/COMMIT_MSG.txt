studies: add 2026-05-15 thinking-budget generalization study

Cross-domain evaluation of Qwen3.6-27B FP8 + MTP=3 under two thinking
budgets (unbounded vs 2048 tokens). Three benchmarks: GPQA Diamond (198),
GSM-Plus 2k stratified, MMLU-Pro 100/subset (1,400). Plus a four-point
budget sweep on GPQA (tb=1024/2048/4096/8192) to disambiguate the
budget-size vs forced-commit confound.

Headline result: thinking_token_budget=2048 strictly Pareto-dominates
unbounded thinking across all measured benchmarks. On GPQA Diamond,
+33.8 pp accuracy (z=6.81, p=1.0e-11) with 0.16x p50 latency; the
unbounded condition hits finish_reason=length on 57.1% of items and
produces zero parseable answers on those. On GSM-Plus, accuracy is a
statistical tie (Δ=+1.1 pp, p=0.38) but tb=2048 is 1.9x faster wall-
clock, 2.7x lower p95 latency, and 2.5x lower p95 output tokens.

Mechanism: the budget triggers a MaxThinkTokensLogitsProcessor (vLLM
#20859) that injects </think> at the budget boundary, forcing the
model to commit to an answer rather than spiral. The four-point sweep
distinguishes whether the win comes from budget size or from the
forced-commit mechanism — see study README for analysis.
